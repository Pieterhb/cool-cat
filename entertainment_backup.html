🌊 Zone 2: 5–15 km (Gordon's Bay, Helderberg & Adventure)

10 to 20 minute drive • Cable skiing, Wine tasting, Nature reserves & Harbors

💦 Blue Rock Adventure Park

📍 8–10.5 km • 12–15 min Paid Activity Tariffs ★★★★★

Built around a crystal-clear, spring-fed lake in Sir Lowry's Pass. Blue Rock is the Western Cape's premier destination for wakeboarding, cliff jumping, scuba diving, and adventure sports.

⚡ Water Sports & Thrills

  • Cable Waterskiing & Wakeboarding: Overhead 5-pulley cable system for beginners through to advanced riders. Equipment rental available on-site.
  • 400m Zipline: High-speed line soaring directly across the deep, spring-fed quarry lake.
  • Rock / Cliff Jumping & Freediving: Designated cliff jumping points into crystal-clear quarry water (up to 60m deep).
  • Scuba Diving: Popular deep fresh-water dive training site (60m deep quarry). Equipment and instructors via local dive charters.
    Indigo Scuba Diving Centre (Gordon's Bay): 📞 +27 (0)83 268 1851 – info@indigoskuba.com
  • Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP) & Canoeing: Hourly board and paddle rentals available.

🍕 Leisure & Dining Facilities

  • The Rock Restaurant & Pizzeria: Wood-fired pizzas, burgers, pasta, and shaded outdoor deck overlooking the lake.
  • Paintball & Beach Volleyball: Outdoor field and sand court available for group bookings.
  • Picnic & Sunbathing Lawns: Day-visitor gate entry fee applies; outside food/beverages restricted by venue policy.
  • Address: Sir Lowry's Pass Road, Somerset West / Sir Lowry's Pass, 7133.
  • 🔔 Booking Tip: Book cable ski and zipline tokens in advance during peak summer weekends and school holidays via bluerock.co.za or WhatsApp.
📞 Blue Rock: +27 (0)21 858 1330 📞 Mobile: +27 (0)63 544 5970 💬 WhatsApp Blue Rock ✉️ info@bluerock.co.za ✉️ bookings@bluerock.co.za ✉️ events@bluerock.co.za ✉️ restaurant@bluerock.co.za 🌐 bluerock.co.za 📞 Indigo Scuba: +27 (0)83 268 1851 ✉️ info@indigoskuba.com 🌐 indigoscuba.com

🍷 Zone 3: 15–30 km (Stellenbosch, Winelands & Markets)

🏛️ Historic Stellenbosch Town, Museums & Markets

📍 25–28 km • 30 min Free Town Access / Museum Fees ★★★★★

South Africa's second oldest town, celebrated for oak-lined avenues, Cape Dutch architecture, boutique art galleries, and vibrant coffee culture.

🏛️ Town Sightseeing & Museums

  • Stellenbosch Village Museum: Four restored heritage homes (Schreuderhuis, Blettermanhuis, Grosvenor House, OM Bergh House) spanning 1709 through the Victorian era. One admission covers all 4 houses.
    Location: Ryneveld Street • Hours: Mon–Sat 09:00–17:00, Sun 10:00–16:30.
  • Dorp Street Walking Tour: Shaded historic street lined with Cape Dutch architecture, art galleries, and boutiques. Features Oom Samie se Winkel (200-year-old Victorian trading store, 📞 +27 (0)21 887 0797).
  • Stellenbosch University Botanical Garden (SUBG): Oldest university botanical garden in SA with bonsai, giant Victoria cruziana waterlilies, and medicinal garden. Small entry fee. Katjiepiering Tea Garden on-site.
    Location: Cnr Van Riebeeck & Neethling Streets • Hours: Daily 08:00–17:00.
  • Jan Marais Nature Reserve: Indigenous fynbos reserve, walking trails, and open-air art sculptures. Free entry for picnics, trail running, and photography.
    Location: Marais Road • Hours: Daily 07:30–18:00 (Winter) / 07:30–19:00 (Summer).

🪢 Cape Canopy Ziplines & Waterfall Quad Buggies

📍 35–40 km • 40–50 min Guided Adventure ★★★★★

Head over Sir Lowry's Pass into the lush Elgin Valley for world-class eco-adventure and adrenaline experiences.

🩲 Cape Canopy Tour (Elgin Valley)

  • World-Renowned Zipline: 13 cliff-side platforms and 320m slides soaring over deep mountain ravines, crossing a suspension bridge above a waterfall.
  • Location: Hottentots Holland Nature Reserve, R321, Elgin / Grabouw.
  • Departures: Daily every 15–30 min between 08:00 and 14:00. Strict advance booking required (allow ~4 to 4.5 hours for full 4x4 mountain drive, 13 platforms, slides, and lunch).
  • Alternative – Houw Hoek Zipline (SA Forest Adventures): Shorter tree-top canopy zipline (ideal for younger families), quad trails, and Palmiet River white-water tubing. Base: Houw Hoek Hotel, N2 Highway, Grabouw.

🐧 Stony Point Penguin Colony & Harold Porter Botanical Garden

📍 ~48 km • 50–60 min CapeNature / SANBI Tariff ★★★★★

Follow Clarence Drive (R44) – celebrated as one of the world's most spectacular cliff-side coastal passes – to Betty's Bay for wild African penguins, amber waterfall pools, and mountain fynbos. Once in Betty's Bay, Stony Point and Harold Porter are only 4.5 km (about 6 minutes) apart, making them an effortless and unforgettable combined day trip.

🐧 Stony Point Nature Reserve (African Penguins)

  • Governing Body: CapeNature
  • Mainland African Penguin Colony: One of only two mainland breeding colonies in South Africa; significantly quieter, less commercial, and more natural than Boulders Beach.
  • Wooden Boardwalk Stroll: Elevated wooden walkways providing safe, close-up viewing of nesting penguins, chicks, cormorants, and dassies against breaking waves.
  • Location: 2411 Coronation Road, Stony Point, Betty's Bay
  • Hours & Entry: Daily 08:00–17:00 (Last entry strictly at 16:30; gates lock at 17:00). Small CapeNature conservation fee (~R30–R40 adults / ~R20 children); strictly cashless / card payments only at the gate.
  • On-Site Dining: “On The Edge Restaurant” at the boardwalk entrance – fresh fish & chips, calamari, coffee, and whale-watching deck.

🌸 Harold Porter National Botanical Garden & Waterfalls

  • Governing Body: SANBI (South African National Biodiversity Institute)
  • Coastal Fynbos Sanctuary: Nestled where dramatic mountain cliffs meet the sea; features landscaped fynbos gardens, natural mountain gorges, and amber-coloured river pools.
  • 🦶 Disa Kloof Waterfall Trail: Gentle, wheelchair- and stroller-friendly 30-minute boardwalk walk to a cascading mountain waterfall and tannin rock pool (home to rare Red Disa orchids blooming Dec–Feb; included with garden entry).
  • 🧗 Leopard's Kloof Trail (Adventure Hike): Exciting mountain gorge hike crossing rivers and climbing timber ladders up to higher waterfalls. (Important: Requires a gate key and trail permit issued at ticket office).
  • Location: Corner of Clarence Drive (R44) & Broadwith Avenue, Betty's Bay
  • Garden Hours: Mon–Fri 08:00–16:30 | Weekends & Public Holidays 08:00–17:00.
  • On-Site Dining: Red Disa Restaurant – breakfasts, light lunches, fynbos teas, and cakes on the mountain terrace.
  • Day-Trip Connection: Located just 4.5 km (6 mins drive) from Stony Point Penguin Colony.
