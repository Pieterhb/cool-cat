Welcome to Cool-Cat Strand

Planning a tasting at the magnificent Waterkloof Wines? Cool-Cat in Strand is the perfect nearby accommodation, offering coastal relaxation after your wine country explorations.

🚗 Getting There from Cool-Cat

Driving Route: Take Broadway Blvd into Somerset West, follow Sir Lowry's Pass Road, and climb up the Schapenberg hills.

Estimated travel time: 15 minutes (9.0 km).
