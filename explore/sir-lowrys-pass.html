Welcome to Cool-Cat Strand

Traveling over Sir Lowry's Pass towards the Overberg? Break your journey at Cool-Cat in Strand for a comfortable, welcoming stopover.

🚗 Getting There from Cool-Cat

Driving Route: Take the N2 highway east straight up the Hottentots Holland mountain pass.

Estimated travel time: 15 minutes (12.0 km).

Destination Highlights

  • Historic mountain pass climbing 420 meters through the Hottentots Holland mountain range.
  • Spectacular summit lookout point overlooking False Bay, Somerset West, and Strand.
  • Popular launch site for paragliding and hang gliding pilots.
  • Gateway to Elgin wine valley, apple orchards, and zipline canopy tours.

💡 Host Insider Tip: Stop at the viewpoint at the top of the pass for an incredible view over False Bay and Strand.

🛏️ Recommended Room: King Arthur Room or Santori Double Room

✨ Best Suited For: Road trippers, paragliders, Overberg travelers, and scenery seekers

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can you paraglide from Sir Lowry's Pass?

Yes, Sir Lowry's Pass is one of South Africa's premier paragliding launch sites when south-westerly or south-easterly breezes blow.
