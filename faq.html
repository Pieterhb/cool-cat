FAQ & Policies

Important information to ensure a safe, relaxing, and enjoyable stay for all our guests.

General Information

What are the Check-In and Check-Out times?

Check-In: From 14:00 (14h00)

Check-Out: At 10:00 (10h00)

Is there free WiFi?

Can I bring my pets?

Unfortunately, no. We do not allow any pets on the premises.

House Rules

Is smoking allowed?

No smoking is permitted inside the rooms. There is a designated smoking area outdoors.

What are the quiet hours?

To maintain a peaceful environment, please lower noise after 23:00. Official quiet hours are between 00:00 and 06:00 (00h00 – 06h00).

Can I bring visitors?

Visitors are allowed by prior arrangement only. Please notify your host in advance.

Payment & Booking Policies

How do I secure my booking?

For standard bookings (more than 7 days in advance & Total > R1,000), a 50% deposit is required upfront to secure your stay. The remaining 50% is due 3 days before arrival or at check-in.

What about short or last-minute stays?

If the total cost of your stay is R1,000 or less, OR if you book within 7 days of arrival, 100% payment is required upfront.

What is the Cancellation Policy?

• More than 30 Days before arrival: 100% Refund (minus admin/gateway fees).
• 14 – 30 Days before arrival: 50% Refund of the total stay cost.
• Less than 14 Days / No-Show: 0% Refund.

Medical & Emergencies

What should I do in a medical emergency?

For immediate private medical response and ambulance service, contact ER24 East Metro at 087 057 5900.

We also have an Emergency First Aid Kit on-site.

Where is the nearest Doctor & Pharmacy?

Legal & POPIA Privacy Compliance

How is my personal information protected (POPIA)?

In accordance with the South African Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), your personal details (name, email, phone number) are collected strictly to manage your reservation, process secure payments, and provide check-in information. Your information is stored securely and is never sold or shared with third parties.

What are your business details (ECT Act Compliance)?

Cool Cat B&B operates in Strand, Western Cape, South Africa. In accordance with Section 43 of the Electronic Communications and Transactions Act (ECT Act), our official registered business address and details are maintained on record and provided on all official booking confirmation receipts.
