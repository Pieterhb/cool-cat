House Rules & Policies

Cool-Cat • Strand, Western Cape

🚭 1. No Indoor Smoking

Smoking is strictly prohibited inside all bedrooms and indoor lounges. Guests are welcome to smoke in designated outdoor garden/patio areas.

🐾 2. No Pets Allowed

To preserve clean, allergen-free suites for all visitors, pets are not permitted on the property.

🤫 3. Quiet Hours (23:00 to 06:00)

Cool-Cat is a peaceful residential retreat. Please reduce noise and television volume after 23:00.

👥 4. Day Visitors

Outside visitors are allowed by prior arrangement with management only.

❌ 5. Tiered Cancellation & Refund Policy (CPA Compliant)

  • More than 30 Days before arrival: 100% Refund (minus 5% bank/gateway handling fee).
  • 14 to 30 Days before arrival: 50% Refund of total booking.
  • Less than 14 Days / No-Show: 0% Refund.
