  • Strand Beach (under 250m): Ideal for swimming in calm False Bay waters, sunrise walks, beachfront jogging, and watching world-class sunsets.
  • Southside Centre (300m): OK Foods supermarket, OK Liquor, ATMs (Nedbank, Standard Bank, Capitec), and pharmacy.
  • Strand Municipal Indoor Heated Pool (250m): Open Mon-Sun 10:00 - 16:45 for lap swimming and fitness.
  • TotalEnergies Service Station (300m): 24/7 convenience store, car wash, and fuel.
  • Takeaways & Fast Food: Nando's Drive-Thru (250m), McDonald's Drive-Thru (300m), Debonairs Pizza, and KFC.
