🏖️ Zone 1: Within 5 km (Strand Local & Beachfront)

Walk or drive 2 to 5 minutes • Surfing, Watersports, Golf & Recreation

🏖️ Strand Beach, Surf School & Watersports

📍 250m to 1 km • 2–5 min Free Public Beach ★★★★★

Strand Beach stretches for kilometres along False Bay with warm, shallow waters and iconic mountain backdrops. Ideal for all ages, sunrise strolls, swimming, and ocean watersports.

🏄 Surfing & Ocean Rentals

  • Son Surf School: Professional surfing lessons for beginners & intermediate surfers.
  • Strand Kayak Rentals: Ocean kayaking & stand-up paddleboards along the promenade (~2 km).
  • Beach Recreation: Swimming, dolphin spotting, shoreline fishing & sunsets.
📞 Son Surf: +27 83 232 4822 ✉️ school@sonsurf.co.za 🌐 surfschools.co.za 📞 Kayaks: 078 605 8236 ✉️ bookings@strandkayakrentals.co.za

⛳ Golf, Paintball & Recreation

  • Strand Golf Club (2.1 km): 18-hole championship coastal links course with driving range & clubhouse.
  • Putt-Putt Mini Golf (1 km): Classic seaside mini-golf fun for kids and families.
  • SWAT Paintball (5 km): Action-packed outdoor tactical paintball.
  • Strand Indoor Pool (250m): Heated municipal pool.
📞 Golf: 021 853 6268 / 021 854 3309 ✉️ bookings@strandgolfclub.co.za 🌐 strandgolfclub.co.za 📞 Putt-Putt: 021 434 6805 📞 SWAT Paintball: 072 453 6999

🌊 Zone 2: 5–15 km (Gordon's Bay, Helderberg & Adventure)

10 to 20 minute drive • Cable skiing, Wine tasting, Nature reserves & Harbors

💦 Blue Rock Adventure Park

📍 8–10.5 km • 12–15 min Paid Activity Tariffs ★★★★★

Built around a crystal-clear, spring-fed lake in Sir Lowry's Pass. Blue Rock is the Western Cape's premier destination for wakeboarding, cliff jumping, scuba diving, and adventure sports.

⚡ Water Sports & Thrills

  • 🏄 Cable Wakeboarding & Waterskiing: Overhead cable system for beginner to pro.
  • 🪂 400m Zipline: High-speed line soaring right across the deep lake.
  • 🧗 Rock Jumping & Freediving: Safe cliff jumps into pristine deep water.
  • 🤿 Scuba Diving & Training: 60-meter deep quarry dive training site.
  • 🛶 Canoeing & Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP)

🍕 Leisure & Dining Facilities

  • 🎯 Paintball Arena & Beach Volleyball Court
  • 🍕 Italian Restaurant & Pizzeria: Wood-fired pizzas on the shaded deck.
  • 🧺 Lakeside Sunbathing & Picnic Lawns
  • Booking: Recommended for cable ski sessions and zipline.
📞 021 858 1330 ✉️ info@bluerock.co.za 🌐 Official Site: bluerock.co.za

⚓ Gordon's Bay Harbor, Bikini Beach & Sunset Cruises

📍 10–12 km • 12–15 min Free Sightseeing ★★★★★

A charming Mediterranean-style coastal village nestled against the mountains, famous for sheltered swimming coves, waterfront dining, and sunset boat charters.

🏖️ Beaches & Harbor Attractions

  • Bikini Beach (Blue Flag): Sheltered from the wind with crystal clear warm waters.
  • Gordon's Bay Main Beach: Wide, shallow beach for family strolls and ice cream.
  • Gordon's Bay Old Harbor: Working fishing harbor and fresh seafood eateries.
  • Harbour Island Marina (~11 km): Waterfront restaurants, luxury yachts & cocktail bars.

🚤 Ocean Charters & Scenic Trails

  • Playa Cruises: Romantic sunset champagne yacht charters, coastal dolphin watching & private celebrations.
  • Clarence Drive (Route 44) (~15 km): One of the world's greatest scenic coastal drives.
  • Steenbras Lookout Point (~15 km): Breathtaking cliff-top viewpoint over False Bay.
  • Danie Miller Hiking Trail (~12 km): Gentle contour walk through mountain fynbos.
📞 Playa Cruises: 067 268 4959 ✉️ hello@playacruises.co.za 🌐 playacruises.co.za

🌿 Helderberg Nature Reserve & Helderberg Farm

📍 10–12 km • 15 min Small Entry Tariff ★★★★★

Situated on the slopes of the Helderberg Mountain, offering spectacular nature walks, birdwatching, seasonal strawberry picking, and single-track mountain biking.

🥾 Helderberg Nature Reserve (~10 km)

  • Hiking Trails: Marked routes from easy 1km fynbos walks to the 7-hour West Peak mountain hike.
  • Bird & Wildlife Spotting: Sugarbirds, bontebok, tortoises, and raptors.
  • Picnics & Oak Café: Shaded lawns overlooking the ocean basin.
📞 021 444 9744 ✉️ helderbergnature.reserve@capetown.gov.za 🌐 helderbergnaturereserve.co.za

🍓 Helderberg Farm (~12 km)

  • Strawberry Picking (Seasonal): Hand-pick sweet strawberries fresh from the fields.
  • Mountain Biking & Trail Running: Dedicated farm forest flow tracks.
  • Farm Hiking & Tea Garden: Scenic mountain trails, proteas, and braai spots.
📞 021 855 4308 ✉️ helderbergfarm@mweb.co.za 🌐 helderbergplaas.co.za

🍷 Helderberg Wine Estates (Vergelegen, Lourensford, Waterkloof, Cavalli)

📍 8–14 km • 10–18 min Tasting & Dining ★★★★★

The Helderberg sub-route is world-famous for its maritime climate, 300-year-old historic Cape Dutch estates, modern glass architectural cellars, and equestrian art museums.

🏛️ Vergelegen & Lourensford Estates

  • Vergelegen Estate (~13 km): 300-year-old camphor trees, octagonal garden, historic library & bistro dining.
  • Lourensford Estate (~12 km): Weekend artisan food & craft market, coffee roastery, Millhouse Kitchen, and chocolate pairing.
  • Morgenster Estate (~12 km): Award-winning extra virgin olive oil tastings, Italian varietals & paste pairings.
📞 Vergelegen: 021 847 2100 / 2111 ✉️ Vergelegen Mail 🌐 vergelegen.co.za 📞 Lourensford: 021 847 0030 ✉️ Millhouse Events 📞 Morgenster: 021 852 1738 ✉️ Morgenster Mail

✨ Waterkloof, Cavalli & Ken Forrester

  • Waterkloof Wines (~12 km): Spectacular architectural glass cube restaurant overlooking False Bay, offering biodynamic organic wines.
  • Cavalli Estate (~12 km): World-class equestrian indoor arena, contemporary art gallery, fine dining & estate gardens.
  • Ken Forrester Wines (~10 km): Acclaimed "King of Chenin Blanc".
📞 Waterkloof: 021 858 1292 ✉️ info@waterklooffwines.co.za 🌐 waterkloofwines.co.za 📞 Cavalli: 021 855 3218 ✉️ Cavalli Info ✉️ Cavalli Dining ✉️ Wine Tasting ✉️ Art Gallery ✉️ Stables & Carriages 📞 Ken Forrester: 021 855 2374 ✉️ Ken Forrester Mail

🐆 Cheetah Outreach, Family Splash Park & Lwandle Museum

📍 4–7 km • 6–10 min Entry Fees Apply ★★★★★

Educational wildlife conservation, kids splash waterpark, horseback vineyard rides, and deep local cultural history located just a few minutes from Cool Cat B&B.

🐾 Wildlife & Family Parks

  • Cheetah Outreach (~4 km): Educational conservation facility with personal encounters with adult cheetahs, cubs, and Anatolian shepherd dogs (Open 10:00–17:00).
  • Monkey Town Day & Splash Park (~7 km): Family day park with waterslides, splash pools, kids playgrounds & restaurant (Note: Primate zoo closed permanently in April 2024; now operating as a family splash/play park).
  • Journey's End Vineyards (~15 km): Mountain and vineyard horse riding trails.
📞 Cheetah: 082 872 4007 / 082 623 9960 ✉️ bookings@cheetah.co.za 🌐 cheetah.co.za 📞 Monkey Town: 066 2666 409 ✉️ pro@monkeys.co.za 🌐 monkeys.co.za 📞 Journey's End: 021 200 2698 ✉️ info@journeysend.co.za

🏛️ Lwandle Migrant Labour Museum

  • Lwandle Museum (~6 km): South Africa's premier museum dedicated to documenting the migrant labour hostel system.
  • Educational guided township walking tours and community craft exhibitions.
  • Highly recommended for history, cultural, and international guests.
📞 021 845 6119 💬 WhatsApp: 069 631 5435 ✉️ info@lwandlemuseum.co.za 🌐 lwandlemuseum.co.za

🍷 Zone 3: 15–30 km (Stellenbosch, Winelands & Markets)

20 to 35 minute drive • Cape Dutch heritage, E-Bike tours, Chocolate pairings & Golf

🏛️ Historic Stellenbosch Town, Museums & Markets

📍 25–28 km • 30 min Free Town Access / Museum Fees ★★★★★

South Africa's second oldest town, celebrated for oak-lined avenues, Cape Dutch architecture, boutique art galleries, and vibrant coffee culture.

🎨 Town Sightseeing & Museums

  • Dorp Street Walking Tour: Shaded historic street lined with art galleries, cafés & boutiques.
  • Stellenbosch Village Museum: Four restored heritage homes spanning four centuries.
  • Stellenbosch University Botanical Garden: The oldest university botanical garden in South Africa.
  • Jan Marais Nature Reserve: Tranquil indigenous fynbos park and sculpture garden.
📞 Village Museum: 021 887 2937 ✉️ villagemuseum@stelmus.co.za 🌐 stelmus.co.za

🥐 Artisan Markets & Food Tours

  • Route 44 Market (~25 km): Weekend artisan food stalls, local crafts, craft beer & live music.
  • Foodies on Foot (Bites & Sites): Guided culinary and historical walking tours through Stellenbosch.
  • Curio & Boutique Shopping: Handcrafted leather goods, jewelry, and South African wines.

🍇 Spier, Waterford, Delaire Graff, Tokara & Muratie

📍 22–31 km • 25–35 min Tastings & Tours ★★★★★

Explore world-renowned wine estates offering unique experiences from chocolate pairings and Segway vineyard tours to full moon hikes.

🦅 Spier & Waterford Estates (~22–25 km)

  • Spier Wine Farm: Eagle Encounters raptor rehab, Segway tours, organic wine tasting & river picnics.
  • Waterford Estate: Famous Wine & Chocolate pairing, vintage cellar tours, and wine drive safari.
📞 Spier: 021 809 1100 ✉️ info@spier.co.za 🌐 spier.co.za 📞 Waterford: 021 880 5300 ✉️ info@waterfordestate.co.za 🌐 waterfordestate.co.za

🖼️ Delaire Graff, Tokara & Muratie (~30–31 km)

  • Delaire Graff & Tokara: Monumental bronze sculptures, Laurence Graff private art, panoramic Helshoogte pass views.
  • Muratie & Dirtopia Trail Centre: Sunset & Full Moon vineyard hikes, MTB bike rentals & single-tracks.
  • Delheim & Lanzerac Estates: Historic cellars and wine/food pairings.

🌲 Zone 4: 30–50 km (Grabouw, Elgin Valley, Betty's Bay & Coast)

35 to 60 minute drive • Ziplining, Quad buggies, African penguins & Botanical waterfalls

🪢 Cape Canopy Ziplines & Waterfall Quad Buggies

📍 35–40 km • 40–50 min Guided Adventure ★★★★★

Head over Sir Lowry's Pass into the lush Elgin Valley for world-class eco-adventure and adrenaline experiences.

🪂 Cape Canopy Tour (Elgin Valley)

  • World-Renowned Zipline: 13 cliff-side platforms and 320m slides soaring over deep ravines.
  • Suspension Bridge: Cross high above a natural mountain waterfall.
  • Contact: 021 300 0501 (Advance booking required).
  • Alternative: Houw Hoek Zipline (028 284 9646) in Grabouw.
📞 Cape Canopy: 021 300 0501 📞 Houw Hoek: 028 284 9646

🏎️ Waterfall Buggy & Quad Bike Adventures

  • Cape Town Waterfall Buggy Adventures: Off-road quad biking and mountain buggy trails through pine forests.
  • Waterfall Swim: Stops at secluded natural mountain rock pools.
  • Contact: +27 68 008 3119 (09:00–16:00 daily).
📞 Waterfall Buggies: +27 68 008 3119

🍏 Elgin Valley Apple Orchards, Farm Stalls & Cider

📍 ~38 km • 40–50 min Free / Farm Dependent ★★★★★

South Africa's cool-climate apple and pear capital, surrounded by pine forests, mountain passes, fresh cider tastings, and farm-gate markets.

✨ Elgin Valley Highlights:

  • Peregrine & Orchard Farm Stalls: Famous venison pies, fresh artisanal breads, local preserves, and craft ciders.
  • Cool-Climate Wine Tasting: Acclaimed Pinot Noir, Chardonnay, and Sauvignon Blanc estates.
  • Scenic Mountain Drives & Orchard Photography: Stunning blossom views in spring and harvest in autumn.
🌐 Tourism Portal: elginvalleytourism.co.za

🐧 Stony Point Penguin Colony & Harold Porter Botanical Garden

📍 ~48 km • 50–60 min CapeNature / SANBI Tariff ★★★★★

Follow the dramatic Clarence Drive coastal road to Betty's Bay for wild African penguins, amber waterfall pools, and pristine coastal fynbos.

🐧 Stony Point Nature Reserve (African Penguins)

  • One of only two mainland African Penguin breeding colonies in South Africa.
  • Scenic wooden boardwalk allowing close-up, respectful viewing of nesting penguins, cormorants, and dassies.
  • Significantly less crowded and more natural than Boulders Beach.
🌐 CapeNature: capenature.co.za

🌺 Harold Porter Botanical Garden & Waterfalls

  • Renowned botanical garden where coastal fynbos meets sheer mountain cliffs.
  • Disa Kloof Waterfall Walk: Easy stroll leading to a natural amber rock pool and cascading waterfall.
  • Betty's Bay Main Beach: Vast, unspoiled wild ocean beach backed by dunes.
🌐 SANBI: sanbi.org
