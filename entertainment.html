Fun & Entertainment Within 50km

Strand is the ideal coastal basecamp. Within 50km, your guests have access to pristine beaches, world-renowned wine estates, adrenaline water sports, hiking, animal encounters, and family fun.

🏖️ 1. Strand & Gordon's Bay — Closest Activities

📍 0 to 10 km • 2 to 12 mins

Strand features a long sandy beach with safe swimming waters and a wide range of watersports including surfing, sailing, power boating, paddleskiing, and boardsailing.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Strand & Gordon's Bay] Upload your custom photo of Strand Beach & Gordon's Bay here

🏄 Local Strand Activities (0–5 km)

  • Strand Beach (250m): Swimming, sunbathing, long beach walks & sunsets.
  • Son Surf School (1–2 km): Beginner & advanced surfing lessons.
  • Strand Kayak Rentals (~2 km): Ocean kayaking & paddleboards.
  • Putt-Putt Mini Golf (1–2 km): Classic seaside family fun.
  • Strand Golf Club (~3 km): 18-hole championship golf course.
  • Strand Bowling Club (~3 km): Welcoming lawn bowls club.
  • SWAT Paintball (~5 km): Action-packed outdoor tactical paintball.

⚓ Gordon's Bay & Coastal Charm (~10 km)

  • Bikini Beach (Blue Flag): Sheltered cove with warm water & mountain backdrops.
  • Gordon's Bay Harbor: Seafood restaurants, boat charters & scenic harbor strolls.
  • Crystal Pools Area (~15–20 km): Famous hiking trail with natural rock pools & waterfalls.
  • Kogel Bay (~25 km): Iconic coastal beach along the rugged Clarence Drive route.

💦 2. Blue Rock Adventure Park

📍 ~10.5 km • 15 mins

Blue Rock is one of the top adrenaline and adventure parks in the Western Cape, built around a crystal-clear spring-fed lake. Highly recommended for families, younger guests, and adventure seekers.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Blue Rock Adventure Park] Upload your custom photo of Blue Rock Lake & Cable Skiing here

⚡ Adrenaline & Water Sports

  • 🏄 Cable Wakeboarding & Waterskiing: Overhead cable system for all skill levels.
  • 🪂 400m Zipline: High-speed zip directly over the deep blue lake.
  • 🧗 Rock Jumping & Freediving: Safe cliff-jumping heights into fresh water.
  • 🤿 Scuba & Sport Diving: Deep training quarry for divers.

🍕 Day Out & Leisure Facilities

  • 🛶 Canoeing & Stand-Up Paddleboarding (SUP)
  • 🎯 Paintball & Beach Volleyball
  • 🍕 Italian Restaurant & Pizzeria: Shaded lakeside seating.
  • 🧺 Lakeside Picnics & Sunbathing Lawns

🐎 3. Horse Riding (Journey's End Horseback Rides)

📍 ~15–20 km • 20 mins

One of the most memorable outings for couples and families. Experience horseback trails winding through picturesque vineyards, fynbos valleys, and mountain foothills rather than an enclosed arena.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Horse Riding & Vineyards] Upload your custom photo of Journey's End Horse Riding here

✨ Key Highlights:

  • Scenic Trails: Guided rides through historic wine farms and Helderberg slopes.
  • All Skill Levels: Gentle horses for first-time riders, children, and experienced equestrians.
  • Contact: 082 391 1693 (Advance booking recommended).

🍷 4. World-Class Wine Estates & Pairings

📍 ~10 to 35 km • 15 to 30 mins

Cool Cat B&B is positioned right at the gateway to the world-renowned Helderberg and Stellenbosch Wine Routes. Spend the day tasting award-winning vintages and return to ocean breezes.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Helderberg & Stellenbosch Wine Farms] Upload your custom photo of Vergelegen, Lourensford or Spier here

🍇 Helderberg Wine Route (10–15 km)

  • Vergelegen Estate: 300-year-old camphor trees, historic manor & fine dining.
  • Lourensford Estate: Art market, coffee roastery & chocolate pairings.
  • Waterkloof Wines: Spectacular architectural glass box & biodynamic wines.
  • Morgenster Estate: Premium olive oils, Italian varietals & paste pairings.
  • Ken Forrester: World-acclaimed "King of Chenin Blanc".

🍷 Stellenbosch Wine Region (20–35 km)

  • Spier Wine Farm: Segway vineyard tours, picnics & Eagle Encounters.
  • Delaire Graff & Tokara: World-class art collections, panoramic Helshoogte pass views.
  • Jordan Wine Estate: Bakery picnics and 360-degree cellar tours.
  • Lanzerac, Boschendal & Warwick: Historic Cape Dutch architecture & family lawns.

🥾 5. Hiking & Nature Reserves

📍 ~10 to 30 km • 15 to 35 mins

Surrounded by the Helderberg and Hottentots Holland mountain ranges, guests have direct access to pristine fynbos hiking trails, panoramic coastal summits, and bird watching.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Hiking & Nature Reserves] Upload your custom photo of Helderberg Nature Reserve or Crystal Pools here

🌿 Helderberg Nature Reserve (~10 km)

  • Well-marked trails from easy 1km walks to the 7-hour West Peak climb.
  • Abundant birdlife, tortoises, bontebok, and indigenous fynbos gardens.
  • Oak Café and picnic lawns with sweeping False Bay views.

⛰️ Mountain & Coastal Trails (~15–30 km)

  • Steenbras Dam & Crystal Pools: Dramatic gorge hike to cascading mountain pools (Permits required in summer).
  • Kogelberg Biosphere (Betty's Bay): Heart of the Cape Floral Kingdom.
  • Sir Lowry's Pass Viewpoint: Panoramic ocean vista over the entire False Bay basin.

🪢 6. Ziplining & Canopy Tours (Cape Canopy Tour)

📍 ~35 to 45 km • 40 mins

Named by Lonely Planet as one of the best adventure experiences in the world! Located in the scenic Elgin Valley/Grabouw area, soaring over rugged mountain gorges and waterfalls.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Cape Canopy Tour Zipline] Upload your custom photo of the Grabouw / Elgin Canopy Tour here

✨ Key Highlights:

  • 13 Platforms & 320m Slides: Soar high above untouched Cape valleys.
  • Suspension Bridge: Cross a breathtaking ravine beside a natural waterfall.
  • Contact: 021 300 0501 • Advance booking required.
  • Alternative: Houw Hoek Zipline (028 284 9646) in Grabouw.

🚲 7. Cycling & Mountain Biking Trails

📍 0 to 30 km

The Helderberg and Stellenbosch regions form South Africa's premier cycling haven, boasting world-class road routes, coastal promenades, and extensive single-track MTB networks.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Cycling & Mountain Biking] Upload your custom photo of coastal cycling or Jonkershoek MTB trails here

🚴 Leisure & Coastal Cycling

  • Strand Beach Promenade: Flat, scenic coastal ride right outside our door.
  • Clarence Drive (Route 44): One of the world's greatest scenic road cycling routes.

🚵 Mountain Biking Networks

  • Jonkershoek Nature Reserve (Stellenbosch): Epic single-tracks & mountain descents.
  • Helderberg Trails & Lourensford: Vineyard flow trails for all rider levels.

🐆 9. Animals & Wildlife Encounters

📍 ~4 to 12 km • 6 to 15 mins

Get up close with incredible wildlife and conservation efforts right around the corner from Cool Cat B&B.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Cheetah Outreach & Wildlife] Upload your custom photo of Cheetah Outreach or Monkey Town here

🐾 Cheetah Outreach (~4 km)

  • Global conservation centre dedicated to protecting free-ranging cheetahs.
  • Personal encounters with adult cheetahs, cubs, and Anatolian shepherd dogs.

🐒 Monkey Town Primate Centre (~7 km)

  • Home to over 28 exotic primate species from around the globe.
  • Interactive petting zoo, bird park, and children's play park.

📍 ~2 to 20 km

The Helderberg basin is a world-renowned golfer's paradise. Cool Cat B&B offers secure golf bag storage and early breakfasts for tee-off times.

🎨 11. Culture, History & Museums

📍 ~6 to 25 km

Discover South African history, vibrant local art galleries, and authentic Cape Dutch architecture.

📷 [16:9 Image Window Frame — Culture & History] Upload your custom photo of Lwandle Museum or Stellenbosch historic streets here

🏛️ Lwandle Migrant Labour Museum (~6 km)

  • South Africa's first museum dedicated to the migrant labour hostel system.
  • Educational guided township tours and community craft centre.

🏛️ Historic Stellenbosch Town (~25 km)

  • Stellenbosch Village Museum: Restored houses spanning 4 centuries.
  • Dorp Street: Shaded oak trees, boutique art galleries, and Cape Dutch heritage buildings.
